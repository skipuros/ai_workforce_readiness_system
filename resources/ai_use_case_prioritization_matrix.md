# AI Use Case Prioritization Matrix

## Purpose

This matrix helps instructional designers and business partners evaluate proposed AI use cases before developing learning or performance support.

Use it to select use cases that address a defined business need, support employee performance, meet responsible use requirements, and can be measured.

## When to Use This Matrix

Use this matrix after completing the workforce readiness assessment and before designing role specific learning.

Complete it with input from:

1. Business leaders
2. Employees
3. Managers
4. Information technology
5. Security and privacy teams
6. Legal or compliance partners
7. Learning and development

## Instructional Design Decision

### Selected Use Case

Identify the use case recommended for development.

### Performance Need

Describe the work problem and the required employee behavior.

### Learning Requirements

Identify the knowledge, skills, decisions, and practice employees need.

### Nonlearning Requirements

Document policy, access, workflow, communication, staffing, or technology issues that training cannot address.

### Recommended Learning Approach

Describe the learning pathway, realistic practice, job aids, prompts, coaching resources, and manager support.

### Measurement Approach

Identify how adoption, behavior, work quality, efficiency, and business results will be evaluated.
