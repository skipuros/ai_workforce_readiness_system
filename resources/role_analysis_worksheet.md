# AI Role Analysis Worksheet

## Purpose

This worksheet helps instructional designers analyze how an AI initiative changes a specific employee role, its tasks, decisions, workflows, risks, and performance expectations.

Use the findings to define required behaviors and determine what employees need to learn, practice, reference, and receive from their managers.

## When to Use This Worksheet

Use this worksheet after selecting an AI use case and before designing the learning pathway.

Complete a separate worksheet for each affected role.
