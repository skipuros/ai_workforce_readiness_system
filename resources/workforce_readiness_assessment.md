# Workforce Readiness Assessment

## Purpose

This assessment helps instructional designers determine whether employees, managers, workflows, policies, and support systems are ready for an AI implementation.

Use the results before designing training. The findings help identify learning needs, workflow problems, policy gaps, access issues, manager support needs, and measurement requirements.

## When to Use This Assessment

Use this assessment during project discovery and analysis.

Complete it with input from:

1. Business leaders

2. Employees

3. Managers

4. Information technology

5. Security and privacy teams

6. Legal or compliance partners

7. Learning and development

## Readiness Interpretation

| Average Score | Interpretation | Recommended Instructional Design Response |
|---|---|---|
| 1.0 to 1.9 | Not ready | Pause learning development. Resolve business, access, policy, or workflow gaps. |
| 2.0 to 2.9 | Limited readiness | Conduct additional analysis and stakeholder alignment before development. |
| 3.0 to 3.9 | Developing readiness | Begin a focused pilot while addressing documented gaps. |
| 4.0 to 4.5 | Ready | Design and implement role specific learning and performance support. |
| 4.6 to 5.0 | Strong readiness | Proceed with implementation, measurement, and continuous review. |

## Instructional Design Findings

### Performance Problem

Describe the work problem the initiative is expected to address.

### Priority Roles

Identify the employee groups that need support first.

### Required Behaviors

Describe what employees must do differently on the job.

### Learning Needs

Identify the knowledge, skills, decisions, and practice employees require.

### Nonlearning Needs

Document policy, access, workflow, communication, staffing, or technology issues that training cannot resolve.

### Recommended Learning Approach

Describe the proposed learning pathway, practice activities, performance support, and manager resources.

### Measurement Plan

Identify how adoption, behavior, work quality, efficiency, and business results will be evaluated.
